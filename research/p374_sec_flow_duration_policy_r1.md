# P374 SEC flow-duration causality contract

Scientific source/representation boundary only.

Freeze the P289 nine-ticker sample and SEC companyfacts source. For revenue and net-income flow facts, use only facts with filed date on or before each quarterly as-of date. Classify duration from start/end dates before any concept/accession tie-break: direct quarter 70-120 days, half/YTD 150-210 days, nine-month/YTD 240-300 days, annual 330-390 days. Prefer direct-quarter facts. YTD facts are not economically comparable to quarterly facts and remain unusable until a separate causal same-as-of prior-period differencing rule is proven.

Admission gate: at least 80% of eligible flow selections are unique after the duration classification plus fixed concept precedence, and every ticker/family has at least 20 as-of observations. Failure is DATA/REPRESENTATION failure, not fundamental-model failure. Pass only admits a separate YTD differencing adjudicator. No alpha inference, product/weight/window/threshold search, portfolio ranking, allocation, runtime, broker, or live-trading authority.
